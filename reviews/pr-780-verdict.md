## Findings

- F1 — `jpe-ai-delegation/hooks/register.ts:460` and `:519` interacting with `:578`. Defect: an exit that returns `next(...)` after logging its route line produces a second, contradictory route line when the downstream handler rejects, because the registration catch routes every rejection through `stopRoute` with a fresh event. Concrete input: an Agent call for a native custom agent (definition `---\nmodel: haiku\ntools: mcp__tracker__*\n---`) or a call that falls through to the terminal haiku rung, where the engine's `next()` rejects. Wrong outcome: one routing decision is recorded as two events — e.g. `["haiku","terminal rung"]` followed by `["stopped","routing failed closed"]` with empty `eligible`/`scores` — double-counting the call and misreporting an outcome the caller never received (the catch's FAIL result replaces the routed result). Severity: low.
- F2 — `jpe-ai-delegation/hooks/register.ts:426`. Defect: `logRoute` awaits `$.clock.now()` outside the guard in `logDecision` (164-170), so a clock rejection makes `stopRoute` (433) reject before `failure()` runs and the catch at 578 rejects in turn. Concrete input: an engine whose `$.clock.now()` rejects while any exit logs its line. Wrong outcome: zero route lines and the routing decision is replaced by a hook error. Severity: info (exotic; the fixture clock never rejects and the decision-log write itself is properly guarded).
